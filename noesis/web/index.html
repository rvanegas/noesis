Your first library

A library is where your writing lives on noesis: your chats and documents, and the sources they cite. noesis checks and indexes it, and Claude reads it through noesis’s tools. Start with one; you can make more later.

Your names

The headings in your chats that are you, besides Prompt: in a chat from The Floor, the name you spoke under. Everywhere else in a chat is someone else, Claude or another person. Indexing and Claude’s reading of your library go by these.

Import

A chat or a document in Markdown or text, or a claude.ai export (its zip or conversations.json). It is checked and repaired as Fix would; a chat that cannot be repaired comes in as a document. Each lands in its year’s directory.
